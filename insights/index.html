CONVEYANCING · GUIDE

Understanding the Property Transfer Process

A brief starting point for buyers and sellers in South Africa.

  • Confirm the parties, property description and agreed conditions before signing.
  • Ask which documents and compliance certificates may be required.
  • Plan for registration steps, fees and communication between the parties.

The details of a specific transaction and applicable requirements should be discussed with a conveyancer.

DECEASED ESTATES · GUIDE

Preparing for Estate Administration

Documents and questions to consider after a bereavement.

  • Locate the will and relevant personal records where available.
  • Establish who is responsible for reporting and administering the estate.
  • Make a list of assets, liabilities and any urgent practical needs.

Estate administration is sensitive and fact-dependent. Seek advice on the applicable procedures.

POPIA · GUIDE

A Starting Checklist for Data Protection

General considerations for responsible handling of personal information.

  • Identify what personal information your organisation collects and why.
  • Review notices, access controls, retention and security practices.
  • Assess whether a response process for data requests and incidents is in place.

POPIA duties depend on the organisation, its activities and applicable legal requirements.
